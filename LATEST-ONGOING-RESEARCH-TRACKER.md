## Details

1. **Artemis**
- Artemis II launched 2026-04-01 and splashed down 2026-04-10. It set a human distance record of 406,771 km.
- Artemis III was redesigned in Feb 2026 as an Earth-orbit docking test with Starship HLS / Blue Moon; NASA is targeting summer 2027.
- Sources:
  - https://engadget.com/science/space/the-artemis-ii-astronauts-are-back-after-a-10-day-journey-around-the-moon-033800654.html
  - https://aerospaceamerica.aiaa.org/nasa-eyeing-year-end-tanking-test-and-summer-2027-launch-for-artemis-iii/

2. **Gaganyaan**
- G1 has slipped from March 2026 to H2, then Q4 2026. Crew module–service module separation ground tests were completed in mid-October 2026.
- Sources:
  - https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2026/10/17/are-we-closer-to-uncrewed-gaganyaan-launch-mission-clears-key-test.amp.html
  - https://spacelaunchnow.app/launch/lvm-3-gaganyaan-1

3. **Chandrayaan-4 / Chandrayaan-5 (LUPEX)**
- Chandrayaan-4 (sample return) was approved in Sep 2024. LUPEX was sanctioned on 2025-03-10, with an ISRO lander and JAXA rover. ISRO says both will fly by 2028.
- Sources:
  - https://www.isro.gov.in/ISRO%5FEN/ISRO_JAXA_CH5_Technical_Interface_Meet.html
  - https://spacenews.com/?p=507567

4. **Europa Clipper**
- The Earth flyby is also the only absolute in-flight calibration of the magnetometer.
- Source: https://doi.org/10.5194/epsc2026-284

5. **Tianwen-2**
- Early images suggest an ~18 m, ordinary-silicate body, which challenges the lunar-ejecta origin idea.
- Source: https://www.astronomy.com/space-exploration/tianwen-2-arrives-at-target-asteroid-kamo%CA%BBoalewa/

6. **Rubin LSST**
- ~10 TB/night and up to ~7 million alerts/night.
- Source: https://www.ifae.es/news/2026/06/30/action-nsfdoe-vera-c.-rubin-observatory-begins-the-greatest-cosmic-movie-ever-made/

7–9. See [`cosmology/cosmology-core.md`](cosmology/cosmology-core.md) and [`astrobiology/biosignatures-and-habitability.md`](astrobiology/biosignatures-and-habitability.md).
