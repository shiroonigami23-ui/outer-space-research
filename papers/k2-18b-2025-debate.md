# The K2-18 b DMS debate (2025): Madhusudhan et al. vs independent reanalyses

- **Papers:**
  - Madhusudhan et al. 2025, *ApJL* (April 2025): JWST MIRI evidence for DMS/DMDS.
  - NASA-led joint reanalysis (arXiv, July 2025).
  - "Sub-Neptune K2-18b does not meet the standards of evidence for life", *The Astronomical Journal* (Oct 2025).
- **Access:** 🔓 mostly open/arXiv
- **Analysis basis:** abstracts, *AJ* abstract text and coverage (National Geographic, Astronomy.com, astrobiology.com).

## The claim
- MIRI mid-IR transmission features were interpreted as DMS and/or DMDS at ~3σ. On Earth DMS is made by marine life, which supported a "Hycean" ocean-world reading.

## The critiques
1. **Signal authenticity (AJ 2025):**
   - Independent reductions show MIRI spectra are dominated by unresolved instrument systematics (red noise).
   - Different wavelength binning gives "a potpourri of planet spectra".
   - **87.5%** of retrievals with the critics' preferred binning do *not* favour DMS/DMDS.
   - In the full 0.7–12 μm spectrum, CH₄ is confirmed, CO₂ is favoured, and DMS is interchangeable with C₂H₄.
2. **Context (NASA-led):** combining four new transits with the original data, three teams found no conclusive DMS. Modelling shows **non-biological** chemistry can make DMS in H₂-rich atmospheres.
3. **Bigger picture:** "a single potential biosignature would not constitute discovery of life" (NASA).

## My Take
The lesson is about **instrument systematics plus context**. The DMS story failed CoLD step 2 (is the signal real?) before it ever reached step 4 (non-biological sources). This is a good template for judging future JWST biosignature claims.
