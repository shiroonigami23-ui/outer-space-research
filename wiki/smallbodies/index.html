☄️ Asteroids & Comets

Asteroids, comets and interstellar objects: sizes, orbits, discoveries and the missions that visited them.

29 entries in 3 groups.

📊 Charts and statistics for this catalog
Asteroids

Vesta

A protoplanet with a huge south-pole impact basin; source of HED meteorites; orbited by Dawn.

Asteroids

Pallas

Third-largest asteroid, on a steeply tilted orbit.

Asteroids

Hygiea

Nearly round; could qualify as a dwarf planet.

Asteroids

Psyche

Possibly the exposed core of a protoplanet; NASA's Psyche arrives in 2029.

Asteroids

Bennu

Carbon-rich rubble pile sampled by OSIRIS-REx; samples hold amino acids and all five nucleobases (2025).

Asteroids

Ryugu

Sampled by Hayabusa2 (returned 2020).

Asteroids

Itokawa

First asteroid sample return (Hayabusa, 2010).

Asteroids

Eros

First asteroid orbited and landed on (NEAR Shoemaker, 2001).

Asteroids

Apophis

Will pass within ~32,000 km of Earth on 13 April 2029, closer than geostationary satellites; no impact risk for 100+ years.

Asteroids

Didymos

Target of DART's 2022 impact on its moon Dimorphos; ESA's Hera arrives 2026.

Asteroids

Kamoʻoalewa

A possible fragment of the Moon that circles near Earth; China's Tianwen-2 has been studying it since July 2026.

Asteroids

2024 YR4

Briefly had a ~3% chance of hitting Earth in 2032 before being ruled out; still a small chance to hit the Moon.

Asteroids

Arrokoth

A pristine 'snowman' contact binary visited by New Horizons (2019).

Asteroids

Sedna

One of the most distant known objects, taking ~11,000 years to orbit.

Asteroids

Gonggong

A large reddish dwarf-planet candidate with a moon.

Asteroids

Quaoar

Has a ring far outside where theory says rings can exist.

Asteroids

Chicxulub impactor

The ~10 km asteroid that hit Mexico 66 million years ago and ended the dinosaurs.

Comets

Halley's Comet

The most famous comet, recorded since 240 BCE; returns in 2061.

Comets

Comet Hale–Bopp

One of the brightest comets of the 20th century, visible for 18 months.

Comets

Comet 67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko

Orbited by Rosetta and landed on by Philae (2014).

Comets

Comet Shoemaker–Levy 9

Broke apart and smashed into Jupiter in July 1994.

Comets

Comet NEOWISE

The brightest Northern Hemisphere comet since Hale-Bopp.

Comets

Comet Tsuchinshan–ATLAS

The bright 'comet of the year' in October 2024.

Comets

Comet Tempel 1

NASA's Deep Impact fired a projectile into it in 2005.

Comets

Comet Wild 2

Stardust collected its dust and returned it in 2006.

Comets

Comet Encke

The shortest-period known major comet.

Interstellar objects

ʻOumuamua

The first known interstellar visitor; cigar- or pancake-shaped, with non-gravitational acceleration.

Interstellar objects

2I/Borisov

The first clearly cometary interstellar visitor.

Interstellar objects

3I/ATLAS

The third interstellar object, possibly ~7 billion years old; perihelion 29 October 2025.
