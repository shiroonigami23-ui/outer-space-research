# 📖 Glossary — space terms used in this hub

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AU** | Astronomical unit, the Earth–Sun distance ≈ 149.6 million km |
| **BAO** | Baryon acoustic oscillations: a ~150 Mpc "standard ruler" imprinted in galaxy clustering |
| **Biosignature** | A substance or pattern that life could produce; evidence only after non-biological routes are ruled out |
| **CMB** | Cosmic microwave background, light from ~380,000 years after the Big Bang |
| **CoLD scale** | NASA's 7-step Confidence of Life Detection ladder (Green et al. 2021) |
| **CME** | Coronal mass ejection: a burst of solar plasma and magnetic field |
| **Δv (delta-v)** | Change in velocity a spacecraft must make; the "currency" of mission design |
| **Eccentricity (e)** | Orbit shape: 0 = circle, <1 ellipse, 1 = parabola (escape), >1 hyperbola (unbound) |
| **H₀ (Hubble constant)** | Today's expansion rate of the universe, in km/s/Mpc |
| **Habitable zone** | Orbital distances where liquid surface water is possible; scales with √(stellar luminosity) |
| **Hohmann transfer** | Two-burn, half-ellipse transfer between circular orbits; minimum-Δv for most cases |
| **Interstellar object** | A body on an unbound (e > 1) path from outside the Solar System |
| **Kerr black hole** | Rotating black hole described completely by mass and spin |
| **Limb darkening** | A star looks dimmer near its edge; it shapes transit light curves |
| **LSST** | Rubin Observatory's 10-year Legacy Survey of Space and Time (began June 2026) |
| **ΛCDM** | Standard cosmology: cosmological constant (Λ) + cold dark matter |
| **Phase angle (launch)** | How far ahead the target planet must be at departure for a transfer to arrive on time |
| **Quasi-moon** | An asteroid co-orbiting the Sun with Earth that appears to loop around it (e.g. Kamoʻoalewa) |
| **Redshift (z)** | Stretching of light by cosmic expansion; 1+z = scale factor now / then |
| **Ringdown** | Final phase of a black-hole merger as the remnant settles, emitting characteristic tones |
| **σ (sigma)** | Statistical significance; 5σ is the usual discovery threshold in physics |
| **Synodic period** | Time between repeat alignments of two orbiting bodies, e.g. Earth–Mars launch windows ~26 months |
| **Transit method** | Detecting planets by the dip in starlight as they cross their star; depth ≈ (Rp/R★)² |
| **TRGB / JAGB** | Tip of the red-giant branch / J-region AGB stars: standard candles used instead of Cepheids |
| **w0–wa** | Two-parameter model of how dark energy's equation of state changes with time |
