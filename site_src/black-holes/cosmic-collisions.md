| Scale | Example | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Planet formation | **Theia** hitting the young Earth ~4.5 billion years ago | Formed the Moon |
| Asteroid impacts | **Chicxulub**, 66 million years ago | Mass extinction, end of the dinosaurs |
| Comet into a planet | **Shoemaker-Levy 9** into Jupiter, 1994 | Earth-sized dark scars on Jupiter |
| Spacecraft into an asteroid | **DART** into Dimorphos, 2022 | Orbit shortened by 33 minutes, first planetary-defence test |
| Star mergers | **V1309 Scorpii**, 2008 | A 'red nova' |
| Neutron stars | **GW170817**, 2017 | A kilonova that forged gold and platinum |
| Black holes | **GW150914**, 2015 | Three Suns' worth of energy released as gravitational waves |
| Galaxies | **Antennae Galaxies**; Milky Way and Andromeda in the future | Starbursts, new elliptical galaxies |
| Galaxy clusters | **Bullet Cluster**, **El Gordo** | Key evidence for dark matter |

## Planetary defence

NASA, ESA and others track near-Earth objects. Asteroid **Apophis** will pass within ~32,000 km of Earth on 13 April 2029 (no risk of impact). **2024 YR4** briefly had a ~3% impact probability for 2032 before being ruled out. NASA's NEO Surveyor (launch ~2027) and the Rubin Observatory will find many more.

## Why stars in colliding galaxies rarely hit

Stars are tiny compared with the gaps between them. When the Milky Way and Andromeda merge, almost no stars will collide, but gas clouds will crash together and trigger bursts of new stars.
