## The nebular theory

1. **~4.6 billion years ago** a cloud of gas and dust collapsed, possibly triggered by a nearby supernova (short-lived isotopes like aluminium-26 in meteorites hint at this).
2. The cloud spun up into a flat **protoplanetary disk** around the young Sun. ALMA now images such disks around other stars, with gaps carved by forming planets.
3. **Dust grains stuck together** into pebbles, then kilometre-sized **planetesimals**, then protoplanets.
4. Inside the **snow line** (~2.7 AU) only rock and metal could condense, making small rocky planets. Beyond it, ice was plentiful, so cores grew big enough to grab hydrogen and helium: the gas giants.
5. **Giant impacts** finished the rocky planets; one created the Moon.

## The planets migrated

The **Nice model** and **Grand Tack** propose that Jupiter and Saturn wandered, scattering asteroids and comets, possibly causing the Late Heavy Bombardment and delivering water to Earth.

## Leftovers

- **Asteroid belt:** debris Jupiter prevented from forming a planet.
- **Kuiper Belt:** icy bodies beyond Neptune, including Pluto and Arrokoth.
- **Oort Cloud:** a vast shell of comets reaching perhaps 100,000 AU.

## Other solar systems are different

Exoplanet discoveries showed that our system is not typical: hot Jupiters, super-Earths and sub-Neptunes (missing here) are common. See [Types of planets](/wiki/planets/types-of-planets.html).
