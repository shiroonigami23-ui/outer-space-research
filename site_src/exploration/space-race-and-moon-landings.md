## Returning: Artemis

- **Artemis I** (2022): uncrewed test around the Moon.
- **Artemis II** (1–10 April 2026): Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch and Jeremy Hansen flew around the Moon, the first crew beyond low Earth orbit since 1972, setting a human distance record of 406,771 km.
- **Artemis III** was reshaped in 2026 into an Earth-orbit docking test with landers (~mid-2027); the first new landing is targeted for **Artemis IV (~2028)**.
- China plans crewed landings by 2030, and India plans a crewed Moon landing by 2040.

## Why go back?

Water ice in permanently shadowed craters near the south pole could supply drinking water, oxygen and rocket fuel. Chandrayaan-1's instruments helped confirm water on the Moon, and Chandrayaan-3 landed near the south pole in 2023.
