# About and Methods

## Editorial approach

- Facts are sourced from NASA, ESA, ISRO, JAXA, CNSA, peer-reviewed papers and observatory releases. The [All sources](/wiki/sources.html) page lists every reference.
- Speculative ideas (white holes, wormholes, unconfirmed biosignatures) are clearly marked with warning or debate boxes.
- The live tracker and research pages are dated, and they say what changed.

## Photos, overviews and sounds

Catalog photos and overview text load live from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, credited with links to the licence. Sounds come from openly licensed recordings and NASA sonifications on Wikimedia Commons; where none exists the page says so honestly (space is a vacuum, so 'sounds of space' are radio, plasma or data converted into audio). The [Photo status](/wiki/photo-status.html) page lists any entry still without a picture, with a ready-made image-generation prompt.
