🌙 Moons

The most important moons in the solar system, from Earth's Moon to ocean worlds like Europa and Enceladus.

31 entries in 5 groups.

📊 Charts and statistics for this catalog
Moons of Earth & Mars

Moon

Earth's only natural satellite, probably formed from a giant impact ~4.5 billion years ago; it stabilises Earth's tilt and drives tides.

Moons of Earth & Mars

Phobos

A potato-shaped moon spiralling inward; it will break apart or crash into Mars in ~50 million years.

Moons of Earth & Mars

Deimos

Mars's smaller, outer moon, smooth and dusty.

Moons of Jupiter

Io

The most volcanically active body in the solar system, heated by tidal flexing.

Moons of Jupiter

Europa

An ice-covered moon with a salty ocean beneath, a prime target in the search for life (Europa Clipper arrives 2030).

Moons of Jupiter

Ganymede

The largest moon in the solar system, bigger than Mercury and with its own magnetic field; target of ESA's JUICE.

Moons of Jupiter

Callisto

The most heavily cratered object in the solar system, possibly with a deep ocean.

Moons of Jupiter

Amalthea

A small red inner moon that sheds dust into Jupiter's rings.

Moons of Jupiter

Himalia

The largest of Jupiter's irregular outer moons.

Moons of Saturn

Titan

The only moon with a thick atmosphere and lakes of liquid methane and ethane; Huygens landed there in 2005.

Moons of Saturn

Enceladus

Sprays water geysers from a global subsurface ocean that contains salts, organics and phosphorus.

Moons of Saturn

Mimas

'Death Star' moon with the giant Herschel crater; may hide a young ocean.

Moons of Saturn

Iapetus

Two-toned moon, one side dark as coal and the other bright as snow, with an equatorial ridge.

Moons of Saturn

Rhea

Saturn's second-largest moon, an icy, cratered world.

Moons of Saturn

Dione

Icy moon with bright ice cliffs.

Moons of Saturn

Tethys

Almost pure water ice, with the vast Odysseus crater.

Moons of Saturn

Hyperion

A sponge-like moon that tumbles chaotically.

Moons of Saturn

Phoebe

A captured Kuiper Belt object orbiting backwards.

Moons of Saturn

Pan

A 'ravioli'-shaped shepherd moon in the Encke Gap.

Moons of Uranus

Miranda

A patchwork moon with the tallest cliff known, Verona Rupes (~20 km).

Moons of Uranus

Ariel

The brightest Uranian moon, with young canyons.

Moons of Uranus

Umbriel

The darkest major moon of Uranus.

Moons of Uranus

Titania

Uranus's largest moon, discovered by William Herschel.

Moons of Uranus

Oberon

Outermost large moon of Uranus, heavily cratered.

Moons of Neptune & beyond

Triton

A captured Kuiper Belt object orbiting backwards, with nitrogen geysers seen by Voyager 2.

Moons of Neptune & beyond

Nereid

Has one of the most eccentric orbits of any moon.

Moons of Neptune & beyond

Proteus

A dark, box-shaped moon.

Moons of Neptune & beyond

Charon

Half Pluto's size, with a red north pole stained by gas escaping from Pluto.

Moons of Neptune & beyond

Dysnomia

Eris's moon, used to measure the dwarf planet's mass.

Moons of Neptune & beyond

Dactyl

The first moon of an asteroid ever found (by Galileo).

Moons of Neptune & beyond

Dimorphos

The moonlet NASA's DART spacecraft hit in 2022, shortening its orbit by 33 minutes, the first planetary-defence test.
