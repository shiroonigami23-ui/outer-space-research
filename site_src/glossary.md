# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Accretion disk** | Swirling, heated matter spiralling into a star or black hole |
| **Astronomical unit (AU)** | Average Earth–Sun distance, ~150 million km |
| **Big Bang** | The hot, dense early state from which the universe expanded 13.8 billion years ago |
| **Black hole** | Region where gravity prevents anything, even light, from escaping |
| **Brown dwarf** | Object between a giant planet and a star that cannot sustain hydrogen fusion |
| **Cepheid** | Pulsating star whose period reveals its luminosity |
| **CMB** | Cosmic microwave background, the Big Bang's afterglow |
| **Comet** | Icy body that develops a coma and tails near the Sun |
| **Constellation** | One of 88 official regions of the sky |
| **Dark energy** | Unknown cause of the universe's accelerating expansion (~68%) |
| **Dark matter** | Invisible matter detected by gravity (~27%) |
| **Dwarf planet** | Round body orbiting the Sun that has not cleared its orbit (Pluto, Ceres) |
| **Event horizon** | Boundary of a black hole beyond which escape is impossible |
| **Exoplanet** | Planet orbiting a star other than the Sun |
| **Galaxy** | System of stars, gas, dust and dark matter bound by gravity |
| **Gravitational wave** | Ripple in spacetime from accelerating masses |
| **Habitable zone** | Distance from a star where liquid water could exist on a planet's surface |
| **Hubble constant (H₀)** | Current expansion rate of the universe (~67–73 km/s/Mpc) |
| **Kilonova** | Explosion from merging neutron stars that forges heavy elements |
| **Kuiper Belt** | Region of icy bodies beyond Neptune |
| **Lagrange point** | Spot where gravity and orbital motion balance (e.g. L1, L2) |
| **Light-year** | Distance light travels in a year, ~9.46 trillion km |
| **Magnetar** | Neutron star with an extreme magnetic field |
| **Magnitude** | Brightness scale; lower numbers are brighter |
| **Meteorite** | Space rock that reaches the ground |
| **Nebula** | Cloud of gas and dust |
| **Neutron star** | Ultra-dense collapsed core of a massive star |
| **Oort Cloud** | Distant shell of comets around the solar system |
| **Parallax** | Apparent shift of a star used to measure its distance |
| **Parsec** | 3.26 light-years |
| **Pulsar** | Rotating neutron star emitting beams of radiation |
| **Quasar** | Extremely bright active galactic nucleus powered by a black hole |
| **Redshift (z)** | Stretching of light by expansion or motion; higher z means farther and earlier |
| **Singularity** | Point of infinite density predicted inside a black hole |
| **Sonification** | Turning data into sound |
| **Supernova** | Explosive death of a star |
| **Transit** | A planet passing in front of its star |
| **White dwarf** | Earth-sized remnant of a Sun-like star |
| **White hole** | Hypothetical time-reverse of a black hole |
| **Wormhole** | Hypothetical tunnel through spacetime |
