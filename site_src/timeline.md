# Timeline

## The universe

<div class="timeline">
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">13.8 bn yr ago</span>: Big Bang and inflation.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">+3 min</span>: Hydrogen and helium nuclei form.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">+380,000 yr</span>: Atoms form; the CMB is released.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">+~100–200 Myr</span>: First stars ignite.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">+~280 Myr</span>: MoM-z14, the most distant galaxy seen so far.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~13 bn yr ago</span>: The Milky Way's oldest stars form.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~10 bn yr ago</span>: 'Cosmic noon', the peak of star formation.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">4.6 bn yr ago</span>: The Sun and solar system form.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">4.5 bn yr ago</span>: A giant impact forms the Moon.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~3.8 bn yr ago</span>: Earliest evidence of life on Earth.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">66 Myr ago</span>: The Chicxulub impact ends the dinosaurs.</div>
</div>

## Astronomy and spaceflight

<div class="timeline">
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">499 CE</span>: Aryabhata proposes that Earth rotates.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1054</span>: The Crab supernova is recorded.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1543</span>: Copernicus publishes the Sun-centred model.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1608–10</span>: Telescope invented; Galileo discovers Jupiter's moons.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1609–19</span>: Kepler's laws of planetary motion.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1687</span>: Newton's laws of motion and gravitation.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1781</span>: Herschel discovers Uranus.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1846</span>: Neptune is found by prediction.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1915</span>: Einstein's general relativity.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1929</span>: Hubble shows the universe is expanding.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1930</span>: Pluto discovered; Chandrasekhar's limit.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1957</span>: Sputnik 1.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1961</span>: Gagarin, the first human in space.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1965</span>: The CMB is discovered.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1967</span>: Pulsars discovered by Jocelyn Bell Burnell.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1969</span>: Apollo 11 lands on the Moon.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1975</span>: India's Aryabhata satellite.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1977</span>: Voyagers launched.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1990</span>: Hubble Space Telescope.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1995</span>: First planet around a Sun-like star (51 Pegasi b).</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1998</span>: Accelerating expansion discovered; ISS construction begins.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2012</span>: Voyager 1 enters interstellar space; Curiosity lands.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2014</span>: Mangalyaan reaches Mars; Philae lands on a comet.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2015</span>: First gravitational waves; New Horizons at Pluto.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2019</span>: First image of a black hole (M87*).</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2021</span>: JWST launches; Ingenuity flies on Mars.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2022</span>: DART deflects an asteroid; Sgr A* imaged.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2023</span>: Chandrayaan-3 lands near the lunar south pole; gravitational-wave background found.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2024</span>: Chang'e 6 returns far-side samples; Europa Clipper launches.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2025</span>: 3I/ATLAS discovered; GW250114; DESI dark-energy hint; NISAR launches.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2026</span>: Artemis II flies around the Moon (April); Rubin survey begins (June); Roman launches (August); Starship reaches orbit (September); BepiColombo arrives at Mercury (21 November).</div>
</div>
