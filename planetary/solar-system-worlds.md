# Solar System Worlds

Status date: November 15, 2026.

## Mars

- **Context**
  - Jezero Crater held a lake more than 3.5 billion years ago. Perseverance has been exploring it since February 2021.
- **Sapphire Canyon (sample 25)**
  - Drilled in July 2024 from the rock "Cheyava Falls" in the Bright Angel formation on the edge of Neretva Vallis.
  - Shows "leopard spots": concentric reaction fronts where redox-sensitive minerals sit next to organic matter.
  - Published in *Nature* on 2025-09-10 ("Redox-driven mineral and organic associations in Jezero Crater, Mars") and described by NASA as a **potential biosignature**, not a detection of life.
  - The decisive tests need the core back on Earth, and the Mars Sample Return architecture is being restructured.
- **Open question**: can non-biological low-temperature chemistry make the same textures? (See the [`../astrobiology/`](../astrobiology/) track.)

## Europa (Jupiter)

- An ice shell over a global salty ocean, kept liquid by tidal heating.
- **Europa Clipper**: launched 2024-10-14, Mars gravity assist on 2025-03-01, **Earth gravity assist on 2026-12-03**, arrives at Jupiter in April 2030 for 49 planned Europa flybys. It is a Jupiter orbiter, not a lander.
- ESA's JUICE is also on its way to Jupiter, which allows coordinated science.

## Enceladus (Saturn)

- Its south-polar plumes spray ocean water into space, and Cassini sampled them directly.
- Organics, salts and phosphates have been detected in that material. This is the most accessible ocean in the Solar System.

## Titan (Saturn)

- Thick nitrogen atmosphere, methane rain, hydrocarbon lakes, and a water ocean under the crust.
- NASA's Dragonfly rotorcraft is in development to study prebiotic chemistry on the surface.

## Kamoʻoalewa (near-Earth quasi-moon)

- Asteroid 469219 Kamoʻoalewa (2016 HO3) co-orbits the Sun with Earth.
- **Tianwen-2** (CNSA) launched in May 2025 and arrived in **July 2026**, imaging the body from about 20 km.
- Early reports put its diameter at ~18 m and its spectrum closer to an ordinary silicate asteroid. That weakens the leading "lunar fragment" origin idea, but the samples will decide.
- Plan: leave in April 2027, sample capsule returns in late November 2027, then continue on to main-belt comet 311P/PANSTARRS.
