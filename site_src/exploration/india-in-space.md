<div class="timeline">
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1963</span>: First sounding rocket launched from Thumba, Kerala; parts carried by bicycle and bullock cart.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1969</span>: ISRO founded under Vikram Sarabhai.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1975</span>: <b>Aryabhata</b>, India's first satellite.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1980</span>: SLV-3 puts Rohini into orbit; India becomes a launching nation.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1984</span>: <b>Rakesh Sharma</b> flies to Salyut 7.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1993</span>: PSLV debuts, later becoming one of the most reliable rockets.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2008</span>: <b>Chandrayaan-1</b>; its instruments help confirm water on the Moon.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2014</span>: <b>Mangalyaan</b> reaches Mars orbit on the first attempt.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2015</span>: <b>AstroSat</b>, India's first space observatory.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2017</span>: PSLV-C37 launches a record 104 satellites.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2019</span>: Chandrayaan-2's orbiter succeeds; the lander crashes.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2023</span>: <b>Chandrayaan-3</b> lands near the lunar south pole (23 August, now National Space Day); <b>Aditya-L1</b> launches to study the Sun.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2025</span>: <b>NISAR</b> (NASA–ISRO radar) launches; Shubhanshu Shukla visits the ISS; SpaDeX demonstrates docking.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2026</span>: Gaganyaan separation tests complete; uncrewed G1 flight targeted.</div>
</div>

## What's next

Gaganyaan crewed flights (2027), Chandrayaan-4 sample return, LUPEX with Japan (Chandrayaan-5), the **Bharatiya Antariksh Station** (first module ~2028), Shukrayaan to Venus, and a crewed lunar landing by 2040. The private sector (Skyroot, Agnikul) launched India's first private rockets in 2022 and 2024.
