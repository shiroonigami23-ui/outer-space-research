🔭 Telescopes & Observatories

The telescopes that revealed the universe, from Lippershey and Galileo to JWST, Rubin, the Event Horizon Telescope and India's GMRT.

Early telescopes & observatories

Lippershey's spyglass

The first patent application for a telescope, by spectacle-maker Hans Lippershey.

Early telescopes & observatories

Galileo's telescope

Galileo turned a ~20× telescope to the sky and discovered Jupiter's moons, Venus's phases and lunar mountains (1610).

Early telescopes & observatories

Kepler's telescope

Johannes Kepler's design with two convex lenses, the basis of later refractors.

Early telescopes & observatories

Newton's reflector

Isaac Newton's mirror telescope avoided colour fringes of lenses.

Early telescopes & observatories

Herschel's 40-foot telescope

William Herschel's giant; he had discovered Uranus in 1781 with a smaller one.

Early telescopes & observatories

Jantar Mantar

Sawai Jai Singh II's giant masonry instruments, including the world's largest stone sundial.

Early telescopes & observatories

Leviathan of Parsonstown

Revealed the spiral structure of the Whirlpool Galaxy.

Early telescopes & observatories

Yerkes refractor

The largest refracting telescope used for astronomy.

Early telescopes & observatories

Hooker Telescope

Edwin Hubble used it to show galaxies exist beyond ours (1924) and that the universe expands (1929).

Early telescopes & observatories

Hale Telescope

The world's largest telescope for decades.

Modern ground observatories

Keck Observatory

Twin 10 m telescopes that pioneered segmented mirrors and adaptive optics.

Modern ground observatories

Very Large Telescope

Four 8.2 m telescopes; took the first image of an exoplanet (2004).

Modern ground observatories

Gran Telescopio Canarias

The largest single-aperture optical telescope.

Modern ground observatories

Extremely Large Telescope

Under construction; will be the largest optical telescope ever.

Modern ground observatories

Thirty Meter Telescope

Planned giant telescope with Indian participation (site disputed).

Modern ground observatories

Vera C. Rubin Observatory

Began the 10-year Legacy Survey of Space and Time in 2026, with the largest digital camera ever (3,200 megapixels).

Modern ground observatories

Arecibo Telescope

Sent the Arecibo message (1974) and found the first binary pulsar; collapsed in 2020.

Modern ground observatories

FAST

The largest single-dish radio telescope.

Modern ground observatories

Giant Metrewave Radio Telescope

One of the world's largest low-frequency radio arrays, part of pulsar timing gravitational-wave searches.

Modern ground observatories

ALMA

66 dishes imaging planet-forming disks.

Modern ground observatories

Event Horizon Telescope

An Earth-sized virtual telescope that imaged M87* and Sgr A*.

Modern ground observatories

Square Kilometre Array

Under construction; India is a member.

Modern ground observatories

LIGO

First to detect gravitational waves (2015). LIGO-India is being built in Maharashtra.

Modern ground observatories

IceCube

A cubic kilometre of ice watching for neutrinos.

Modern ground observatories

Devasthal Optical Telescope

India's largest optical telescope.

Space telescopes

Hubble Space Telescope

Revolutionised astronomy: the Deep Field, the universe's expansion rate, dark energy evidence.

Space telescopes

James Webb Space Telescope

Sees the first galaxies and analyses exoplanet atmospheres.

Space telescopes

Chandra X-ray Observatory

Images hot gas, black holes and supernova remnants.

Space telescopes

Spitzer

Found TRAPPIST-1's seven planets; retired 2020.

Space telescopes

Kepler

Found over 2,600 exoplanets and showed planets outnumber stars.

Space telescopes

TESS

Surveying bright nearby stars for transiting planets.

Space telescopes

Gaia

Mapped ~2 billion stars in 3D; retired in 2025.

Space telescopes

Planck

Made the definitive map of the cosmic microwave background.

Space telescopes

Euclid

Mapping dark matter and dark energy; first cosmology release in 2026.

Space telescopes

AstroSat

India's first dedicated space observatory.

Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope
